Why Confidant?

Stop spending your time keeping every agent up to date.

When every agent keeps its own notes, you repeat yourself and details drift. Businesses have CRMs. People need a Confidant.

Keep each relationship in one place. The agents you connect can read what you choose to share.

One source for many agents

Connect your agents to one vault. Keep one record per person.

People, not accounts

Remember how you know them, shared places, and moments.

Portable by design

Plain text in Git. Read it, diff it, back it up, or move it to another device.

Packs

Start with relationships. Extend as needed.

The built-in personal pack covers people, places, things, and the moments that connect them. Packs add record types, fields, and checks when you need them.

Get started

Start with your own vault.

Confidant is free and open source. Install it, create a vault, then connect the agents you use.

curl --proto '=https' --tlsv1.2 -LsSf https://github.com/hirefrank/confidant/releases/latest/download/confidant-cli-installer.sh | sh
